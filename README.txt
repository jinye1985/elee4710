IPEM Lab | ELEE 4710 — Complete Course

GET STARTED
Extract the complete ZIP. Open the index.html at the top of this folder directly in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or another modern browser. Keep all chapter folders beside it.
No start.bat, web server, installation or internet connection is required for the course content, equations, animations and annotation tools. External reading and video links require a connection only when opened.

COURSE ORGANIZATION
chapter-01 through chapter-08 each contain their own index.html, references.html and references.json. Chapter pages can also be opened directly. All chapters have links back to the course and to neighboring chapters.

READING GUIDE
Knowledge Point identifies definitions, principles and explanatory knowledge. Blue Interactive Demonstration headings identify models and visual laboratories. Large Example headings separate worked calculations from demonstrations. Click a worked example heading to open its solution.

Use Contents to move within a chapter. Pause motion stops automatic animations; individual labs also have controls. Reduced-motion browser preferences are respected. Font-size buttons change the reading scale.
Pen, Mark, Erase and Undo/Redo are available in the annotation toolbar. Save exports the current viewport and annotations as PNG. Esc returns to reading. Use × to hide the toolbar; Shift+A brings it back. Local browser storage keeps annotations without modifying the HTML file; behavior can vary across local-file browser profiles.

EVIDENCE
Each reference companion maps knowledge and computations to supplied lectures and inspected external sources. Original source mistakes or ambiguous assumptions are identified there. Added numerical cases and curves are explicitly marked as derived teaching models, not measured data. Chapter 5 follows the syllabus topic and the opening electromechanical material in the supplied DC-machine lecture.
Supplied course PDFs are included for personal study. They retain their original copyright; no public redistribution license is asserted. Third-party software licenses accompany each chapter.
