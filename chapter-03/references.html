Source clarifications and corrections

errata-1 L03 p22

Source
L03 p22
Issue
Handwriting converts2.08+j6.91 to7.7Ω and current6.5A.
Correction
Full precision magnitude7.2186676Ω; current6.926486A. The argument is73.2171°.

errata-2 L03 p33

Source
L03 p33
Issue
Numerical Q is negative, but hand-drawn triangle extends upward.
Correction
Capacitive Q leg must point below the real axis.

errata-3 L03 pp44–45

Source
L03 pp44–45
Issue
Delta line/branch labels and magnitude relation are interchanged.
Correction
With branch currents Iab,Ibc,Ica and lineIa into nodea: Ia=Iab−Ica and |Ia|=√3|Iab| for a balanced set.

errata-4 L03 p19

Source
L03 p19
Issue
Current equation handwriting uses V/R while denominator is complex impedance.
Correction
Use I=V/Z.

errata-5 L03 p48

Source
L03 p48
Issue
One-third fuel-to-customer energy statement lacks system/year/technology boundary.
Correction
Do not present as universal/current fact; teach explicit boundary and energy balance instead.
