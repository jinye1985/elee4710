Source clarifications and corrections

errata-1 L04 pp.14–15

Source
L04 pp.14–15
Issue
Reactive power is labeled S in places; rms numerical values are used despite m subscripts; early-rounded reactive-case power is shown as99.7W/var.
Resolution
Use S=P+jQ, |S| in VA, P in W and Q in var, with explicit rms phasors. Exact given loads yield P100W and Q=+/-100var. Instantaneous currents are reconstructed with sqrt(2) times rms magnitude.

errata-2 L04 p.25

Source
L04 p.25
Issue
Printed SC relation appears as Zeq=V1/R1.
Resolution
Corrected to Zeq=Vsc/Isc, dimensions ohms. R=V/R has current units and cannot be impedance.

errata-3 L04 pp.3,6

Source
L04 pp.3,6
Issue
Both negative induced-EMF and positive terminal-voltage derivative forms appear without a complete reference explanation.
Resolution
Body distinguishes induced loop EMF -dλ/dt from passive winding terminal voltage +dλ/dt.

errata-4 L04 p.20

Source
L04 p.20
Issue
The OC current subtraction is a phasor relationship; an ambiguous scalar subtraction is shown/questioned.
Resolution
Use Im=sqrt(Ioc²-Ic²) for orthogonal component magnitudes.

errata-5 L04 p.24

Source
L04 p.24
Issue
Rounding Ic,H to0.045 A and Im,H to0.246 A gives Xm,H≈8940ohm.
Resolution
Full precision raw-data result8949.163ohm in extraction; circuit inspector uses supplied rounded8940ohm clearly labeled as a chosen parameter set.

errata-6 L04 pp.30–33

Source
L04 pp.30–33
Issue
Loaded2200V held as reference; the lagging and leading cases solve different primary supply magnitudes.
Resolution
Do not imply both loaded cases use a common2200V primary. Each regulation comparison holds its separately computed primary supply fixed while unloading.

errata-7 L04 pp.22,32,37

Source
L04 pp.22,32,37
Issue
Measured SC4.55A is rounded and is not exactly the nameplate rated current10000/2200=4.5454545A.
Resolution
Extraction preserves measured4.55A; loading examples use exact rated current with stated rounded series parameters. Result differences are rounding only.
