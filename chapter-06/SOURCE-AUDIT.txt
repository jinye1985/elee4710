MAJOR SOURCE CORRECTIONS
1. The averaged/radial-gap constant2rlB used in the original single-loop numerical exercise must
   not be confused with the sinusoidal emf of one isolated coil rotating in a uniform field.
   These models are distinguished explicitly in both text and the demonstration assumptions.
2. Self-excited generator half-speed answer55V requires fixed flux. At fixed field resistance,
   field current changes too; a new magnetization-curve intersection is required. The source
   graph is insufficient to support a precise corrected self-excited numerical answer, so none
   is invented.55.5V is shown only as a fixed-flux estimate.
3. Maintaining1A field current at50V needs50ohm, incompatible with the given80ohm winding
   plus a nonnegative series rheostat.500rpm result is explicitly conditional on independent
   field excitation or an altered field circuit.
4. Generator reference Ia6A and If1.01A is not external no-load: external output current4.99A.
5. Generator rotational-loss arithmetic corrected to1200−100.6×6=603.6W; efficiencies updated.
6. Motor half-current source typo0.4ohm corrected to0.1ohm; angular-speed units clarified.
7.120A full-load armature current is an explicit exercise convention, not inferred from a
   12kW motor-output rating and100V alone.
8. Source instantaneous-current results neglect inductance. Exact finite-La current at t0+
   is continuous; a separate fast electrical estimate is calculated at approximately unchanged
   shaft speed. The field weakening example also notes finite actual field response.
9. Added-resistor exercise wording corrected from voltage reduced to resistor inserted.
10. All calculations retain full precision until the final displayed answer.

PHYSICAL ORIENTATION AUDIT
Commutator demo uses B along+x, positive rotation counterclockwise as viewed, conductor A
at (r cos(theta),r sin(theta)). Therefore vA×B has z component−omega*r*B*cos(theta).
At theta0, A motional emf points into page and B out. Positive left brush touches B. At
theta180, A emf points out and left positive brush now touches A. Brush voltage remains
positive, with zero at90/270degrees. Colors follow the rotor/segment pair. Dots/crosses mark
emf direction, not assumed load current; the figure explicitly says no load current shown.
The motor explanation separately states F=I l×B and appropriate supply polarity. Signed
equivalent-circuit current points into the top positive armature terminal when motoring and
reverses when generating. Positive angular speed is unchanged while electromagnetic torque
opposes rotation in the generating case.
Compound short shunt is connected after the series coil (series current=line current);
long shunt is before it (series current=armature current). Separately excited loops isolated.
