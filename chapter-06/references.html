C06-M01 Ideal rotating coil and split ring

Id
C06-M01
Title
Ideal rotating coil and split ring
Formula
rA=(r cos theta,r sin theta); vA=(-omega r sin theta,omega r cos theta); B=(B,0); motional emf toward viewer at A proportional to -cos theta; eAB/Epk=-cos theta; brush voltage/Epk=abs(cos theta)
Parameters
Dimensionless waveform, geometric drawing radius only; theta 0–360 degrees.
Assumptions
Uniform field, fixed positive speed, ideal zero-width brushes, zero commutation time, negligible coil resistance. Distinct from radial-field averaged model.
Checks
  • At theta=0: A emf into page, B out; positive left brush touches B.
  • At theta=180: coil emf reverses, positive left brush touches A.
  • At 90 and 270 degrees, emf is zero and contacts change.
  • Every brush emf value nonnegative.

C06-M04 Illustrative self-excitation intersection

Id
C06-M04
Title
Illustrative self-excitation intersection
Formula
E(I,n)=(n/1000)*(3+120*tanh(1.4I)); equilibrium E=Rf*I, solved by bisection.
Parameters
Rf60–230 ohm,n400–1200 rpm. 3V residual,120V amplitude,1.4/A saturation scale explicitly chosen for teaching.
Assumptions
NOT measured data and NOT digitized course graph. Correct field polarity, no armature drop, steady state. Residual voltage makes high-R equilibrium small rather than identically zero.
Checks
  • Residual emf E(0,1000)=3 V.
  • Positive slope decreases with positive I.
  • Equilibrium residual below1e-8 V.
  • Halving speed at fixed field current halves emf; self-excited field current generally changes.
