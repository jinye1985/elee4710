SOURCE CORRECTIONS/CLARIFICATIONS
SC impedance expression V/I, not the printed V/R.
OC magnetizing-current magnitude found by orthogonal component subtraction, not scalar magnitude subtraction.
Faraday induced-EMF sign separated from passive terminal-voltage reference.
Reactive power denoted Q; complex power denoted S=P+jQ; magnitudes use rms values.
Original99.7W/var values arise from early rounding of exact100W/var results.
Full-precision Xm differs from original8940ohm early-rounded value; the approximate circuit inspector explicitly uses the stated rounded parameter set. Regulation/efficiency retain the given rounded series values.
Measured SC4.55A preserved for extraction; exact nameplate rated current4.5454545A used for current-fraction load examples.
The two regulation cases hold loaded secondary220V but require different primary supply voltages. Within each case the loaded/unloaded comparison holds that supply fixed.
