Source clarifications and corrections

errata-1 L05, page 9

Source
L05, page 9
Issue
The ideal no-load speed V/(B length) can be mistaken for a loaded operating point.
Correction
It assumes zero opposing mechanical load and friction. The dynamic laboratory separately includes viscous drag.

errata-2 L05, page 10

Source
L05, page 10
Issue
The generator equation switches to a positive outgoing-current reference.
Correction
A single signed current reference is retained; generator operation has negative current.

errata-3 L05, page 12

Source
L05, page 12
Issue
Work = torque × angle is shown without a constant-torque condition.
Correction
General work is the integral of torque with angle. The product applies for constant torque.

errata-4 L05, page 13

Source
L05, page 13
Issue
Radius and the end-field approximation are implicit, and terminal signs need defined references.
Correction
State the 1 m span as a diameter, the ideal radial-gap field and inactive end conductors. Maintain the prescribed rotation when discussing current reversal.

errata-5 L05, pages 6 and 9

Source
L05, pages 6 and 9
Issue
Inductance is omitted, so the immediate current V/R is idealized.
Correction
Keep the zero-inductance case as a static approximation. With finite L, initial current is continuous and its initial derivative is V/L.
