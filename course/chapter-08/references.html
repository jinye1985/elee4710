C08-M4 Power-angle and P-Q locus

Id
C08-M4
Title
Power-angle and P-Q locus
Formula
I=(Ef exp(j delta)−V)/(jXs); P=3VEf sin(delta)/Xs; Q=3V(Ef cos(delta)−V)/Xs.
Parameters
V=230 V/phase, Xs=2 ohm, Ra=0; Ef=160–360 V/phase, delta=−85 to +85 degrees.
Assumptions
Lossless cylindrical rotor; ideal stiff bus; illustrative parameters; saliency, saturation, thermal, protection and transient limits omitted. P-Q arc is a model locus, not a capability rating.
Checks
385 E/delta points checked: S magnitude=3V|I|; P(−delta)=−P(delta); Q(−delta)=Q(delta). At E=V and delta=0, I=0 and PF undefined. At E=280 V, delta=0: P=0, Q=17250 var.

Source clarifications and corrections

The supplied synchronous lecture contains no worked numeric examples. All three added examples are explicitly labeled derived teaching examples with selected values.
MIT Chapter 9 begins with peak-amplitude phasors and a motor current reference. The lesson uses RMS phase magnitudes and outgoing generator current, hence factors of 3 rather than 3/2 and reversed power signs.
MIT Chapter 9 p. 10 per-unit reactive-power line appears to contain sin(delta); its earlier derived reactive-power equation uses cos(delta). The cosine form is independently obtained from 3VI* and used here.
NPTEL Section 3 equation 24 is displayed with an omitted I in one preceding typeset line; the explicit V=Et−IZs equation and diagram establish the intended KVL. The full complex product is used here.
The qualitative open-circuit characteristic has no claimed numeric calibration. Starting ramps/capture are conceptual, not fabricated measured trajectories.
The primary starting notes describe a torque reversal each half cycle. This chapter states the unambiguous sinusoidal torque and zero cycle average without treating the reversal count as a doubled sinusoidal frequency.
All circuits, rotor sketches, inverter symbol and plotted data are original explanatory renderings driven by cited relationships. No copied figure, unverified machine performance data or purported experimental video is included.
